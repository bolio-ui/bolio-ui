## Section

A band of the page that groups related content, with an optional background. It renders a `section` element. Put a `Container` inside it to limit the width of the content.

<Playground
  desc="Without a background, a Section is transparent."
  scope={{ Section, Container, Text }}
  code={`
<Section>
  <Text>A section of the page.</Text>
</Section>
`}
/>

<Playground
  title="Background"
  desc="Use `bg` to paint the whole band, and a Container to keep the content narrow."
  scope={{ Section, Container, Text }}
  code={`
<Section bg="#f5f5f5" style={{ padding: 24 }}>
  <Container>
    <Text>A band with a background.</Text>
  </Container>
</Section>
`}
/>
