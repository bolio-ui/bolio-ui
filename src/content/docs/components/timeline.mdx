## Timeline

List events in order, such as a changelog or an activity feed.

<Playground
  title="General"
  desc="The basic `Timeline` usage."
  scope={{ Timeline }}
  code={`
<Timeline>
  <Timeline.Item title="Order placed" time="Mon, 9:41">
    We received your order and are preparing it.
  </Timeline.Item>
  <Timeline.Item title="Shipped" time="Tue, 14:05">
    The package left the warehouse.
  </Timeline.Item>
  <Timeline.Item title="Delivered" time="Thu, 10:12" />
</Timeline>
`}
/>

<Playground
